Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

Go to your Profile page inside the app, scroll down to the "Danger Zone" section, and tap "Delete Account". Follow the confirmation steps. All your data will be permanently removed.

How do I report or flag inappropriate content?

On the Community (Social) page, each user card has a "Report" button. Tap it, choose a reason, and our team will review the report promptly.

How do I block a user?

On the Community page or within a user's profile, tap "Block User". Their content will be hidden from your feeds immediately, and we will be notified.

I signed in with Google / Apple and can't access my account.

Make sure you are using the same sign-in method you used to create your account. If you continue to have issues, contact us at the email above.

How do I cancel my membership?

Memberships are managed through Stripe. To cancel, reply to your billing confirmation email or contact support and we will process the cancellation.

My data isn't showing up / app is loading slowly.

Try signing out and back in. Make sure you have an active internet connection. If the issue persists, email us with a description of what you're seeing.

Community Guidelines

All users must follow these guidelines when contributing to the FashionDex community:

  • Do not post offensive, discriminatory, or harmful content.
  • Respect intellectual property — only share content you have the right to share.
  • Do not harass or bully other users.
  • Use the Report feature to flag content that violates these guidelines.
  • Repeated violations may result in account suspension or removal.
